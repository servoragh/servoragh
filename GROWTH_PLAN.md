# Servora — Zero-Capital Customer Acquisition & Growth Playbook

## Target: First 100 Customers + 100 Artisans in Tamale

---

## 1. Growth Channels (GHS 0 Budget)

### A. WhatsApp Growth Engine
- **Direct Artisan Web Profile Card:** Every registered artisan receives a personalized URL (`/provider/kwame-repairs-tamale`) with an explicit "Share on WhatsApp" button. Artisans post this on their WhatsApp Status as a digital business card.
- **Customer Request Broadcast:** Customers can share their service request page link (`/requests/req-982`) directly into local Tamale WhatsApp group chats.
- **Referral Program:** Trackable referral codes (`servora.vercel.app/register?ref=KWAME12`) allowing users to invite friends and track network activations.

### B. On-The-Ground Artisan Recruitment (Tamale Hubs)
- **Sakasaka Mobile Phone Hub:** Onboard 20 top phone/laptop technicians directly using the Admin CSV Importer or mobile onboarding wizard.
- **Aboabo Market & Central Market:** Target high-demand electricians, AC repairers, and tailors. Provide physical flyer templates for display.
- **UDS Dungu & City Campuses:** Distribute WhatsApp links across UDS student community groups for room repair, laptop fixing, and smock/fugu tailoring.

### C. Programmatic Hyper-Local SEO
- Programmatically generated indexable landing pages targeting high-volume local searches in Ghana:
  - `/services/electricians/tamale`
  - `/services/phone-repair/tamale`
  - `/services/plumbers/tamale`
  - `/services/tailors/tamale`
  - `/services/cleaners/tamale`
- Structured JSON-LD metadata for Google Search rich snippets.

## 2. Daily Founder "Launch Mode" Execution Checklist

1. Onboard 5 new verified service artisans in Tamale.
2. Review & approve pending verification requests in Admin Panel.
3. Share 3 featured provider profiles on local Facebook/WhatsApp community groups.
4. Follow up with customers on pending request quotes to ensure job completion.
5. Record weekly North Star metric: **Successful Connections per Week**.
