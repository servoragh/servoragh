# Servora — Market Research & Launch Strategy Report
**Location Focus:** Tamale, Northern Region, Ghana  
**Business Model:** Zero-Capital Hyperlocal Service Marketplace  
**Target Market:** Tamale Metropolis & Surrounding Communities  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement

### The Problem
Finding reliable, trustworthy, and skilled service providers (artisans) in Tamale, Ghana is currently a high-friction, risky, and informal process.
* **For Customers:** Discovering an electrician, phone repairer, plumber, or tailor usually relies on word-of-mouth recommendations or unverified listings on social platforms (Facebook groups, WhatsApp status, Jiji Ghana). Scams, overcharging, poor workmanship, and no-shows are common, with zero recourse or rating history.
* **For Service Providers (Artisans):** Skilled mechanics, tailors, phone technicians, and electricians struggle with inconsistent customer flow. Classified sites (Jiji, Tonaton) charge listing fees without guaranteeing lead quality, and social media posts disappear in fast-moving feeds.

### The Servora Solution
**Servora** bridges this trust gap with a zero-cost, mobile-first service marketplace tailored specifically for Tamale:
1. **Simple Request Wizard:** Customers state what they need, select their neighborhood (e.g., Sakasaka, Choggu, Nyohini, Lamashegu), optionally upload photos, and set urgency.
2. **Verified Provider Profiles:** Providers show verified phone numbers, identity badges, completed job counts, ratings, and portfolio photos.
3. **Instant Direct Connections:** Customers compare transparent quotes and contact artisans via direct call or WhatsApp.

## 6. Monetization Strategy (Phased Approach)

### Phase 1: Zero-Capital Launch (GHS 0 Cost to Users)
- Free customer requests
- Free provider profile creation & lead responses
- Objective: Build liquidity, trust, and complete first 100 successful connections.

### Phase 2: Provider Trust Badges & Promoted Listings (Future)
- **Verified Artisan Badge (GHS 20 - 50 one-time):** Small fee covering physical/ID document check.
- **Sponsored Profile Listing (GHS 10 - 30/week):** Featured top spot on category search, clearly labeled "Sponsored".

### Phase 3: Pro Subscriptions & Lead Packages (Future)
- **Servora Pro (GHS 30/month):** Unlimited lead quote submissions, analytics dashboard, SMS notification alerts.
- **Platform Commission (3-5%):** Applied once Mobile Money (MTN MoMo / Telecel Cash) escrow payment flow is activated via Paystack Ghana.

2. **Shareable Provider Web Cards:** Artisans share their custom URL (`/provider/kwame-electric`) on WhatsApp status.
3. **Customer Request Share Button:** Customers can share their service request link (`/requests/req-101`) directly to trusted artisans or local groups.
4. **Local SEO Dominance:** Programmatic SEO routes (`/services/electricians/tamale`, `/services/phone-repair/tamale`) with Schema.org JSON-LD structured data.

## 8. Strategic Justifications & Key Decisions

* **Why Tamale first?** Tamale is the economic capital of Northern Ghana with rapid urban expansion, high smartphone penetration, strong community cohesion, and an underserved digital marketplace space.
* **Why focus on WhatsApp integration over native mobile apps?** Android app downloads require storage space and high data usage. A PWA (Progressive Web App) loaded via web links or shared on WhatsApp is instant, works on low-cost devices, and costs GHS 0 to deploy.
* **Why omit upfront payment gateways in MVP?** Integrating payment processors requires formal business registration, bank accounts, and merchant verification. Connecting users directly first eliminates financial/legal barriers to immediate launch.
